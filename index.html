StoryBot

StoryBot is a personal automation that writes an original short story once a day, narrates it, renders it as a short vertical video, and uploads it to its owner's own YouTube channel.

It is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

StoryBot uses the YouTube Data API only to upload videos to that one channel. It does not read, change, or delete anything else on the account.
